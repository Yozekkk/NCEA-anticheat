# NCEA names and branding

The NCEA Community License 1.0 grants copyright permissions for eligible
material; it does not grant a general right to use NCEA Studio, NCEA AntiCheat,
or NCEA logos as the branding of a third-party product. No claim of registered
trademark status is made.

- An unchanged distribution may retain the original NCEA AntiCheat identity.
- An unofficial modified fork should use a distinct primary name and visual
  identity. It may truthfully state “Based on NCEA AntiCheat” and must retain
  the origin notice required by [LICENSE](LICENSE).
- A fork must not claim NCEA Studio made, approved, or supports its changes.
- Truthful references to the original project, its origin, and compatibility
  are allowed. The original creator must not be obscured.

These rules concern source identification. Third-party artwork and fonts have
their own licenses described in [docs/ASSET_CREDITS.md](docs/ASSET_CREDITS.md).
